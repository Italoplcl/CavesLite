# Herobrine 1.9.1 – Cave Encounters & Combat

- Cave-aware spawn search: 48 attempts, local underground Y, 5–30 block ranges.
- Cave fallback: STALKING -> WATCHER -> CREEPING rather than wasting an encounter.
- Combat health default 80 HP (40 hearts).
- Attack default 2 damage (1 heart) plus 1.15 knockback.
- Combat lasts at most 35 seconds by default.
- At timeout or <=20% health, Herobrine visibly flees for up to 4 seconds, then vanishes.
- Faster plugin-controlled movement for Mannequin.
- No terrain generation/modification. Redstone torches remain the only environmental placement.
